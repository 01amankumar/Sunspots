What changed

Two data bugs fixed, two new architectures added

Data pipeline

  • Wrong file, right name. SN_m_tot_V2.0.csv is SILSO's raw monthly mean (month-to-month jumps up to ~35). The paper's low RMSE requires the actual 13-month smoothed series — found as SN_ms_tot_V2.0 (2).csv in the same folder (mean |Δ| ≈ 2.9/month) and used for every result here.
  • Scaler leakage. The original notebook fit MinMaxScaler on the full series before splitting train/test, leaking future min/max into training. Scalers here are fit on train only.
  • Provisional rows dropped. The 4 most-recent months carry SILSO's "not yet definitive" flag (smoothing needs ±6 months of future data); these are excluded rather than kept.

Modeling

  • PatchTST-style transformer. RevIN-normalized input window split into overlapping patches, embedded as tokens, encoded with a 2-layer transformer encoder — a genuine patch-based time-series transformer (Nie et al., 2023), not a relabeled RNN.
  • RevIN on the LSTM baseline too. Per-window instance normalization instead of one global scaler, since sunspot amplitude varies ~3× between weak and strong cycles.
  • Chronos-Bolt, zero-shot. Amazon's real pretrained time-series foundation model, run with no training on this data at all, for reference — RMSE 3.82, already ahead of naive persistence with zero fitting. (The paper's notebook had columns labeled "ChronosModel" / "TimesFM" / "Moirai" / "TimeGPT" that were, on inspection, the same plain Keras layers under different names — not real foundation-model runs.)
